About Kuip

A place for everything.

Kuip is a community platform where people find others who care about the same things, and share what they know, love and wonder about.

Why we're building it

The internet is better with your people in it.

Good conversations, useful tips and great laughs are scattered everywhere. Kuip brings them together in one place that's easy to join and easy to enjoy.

Who it's for

Everyone.

Students, developers, gamers, creatives, entrepreneurs, hobbyists and the plain curious. Young or old, serious or silly.

What we believe

Three simple ideas.

👋

Everyone's welcome

You don't need to be an expert or a regular. Show up, say something, find your people.

🔒

Your posts, your control

Every post is tied to its author, and only the author can edit or delete it.

🎉

Fun is a feature

Useful and entertaining aren't opposites. The best communities are both.

Where we are. Kuip is in early access and built in Nigeria. Sign-up, posting and editing work today, with communities, comments and more on the way.
