How it works

Simple on purpose.

Kuip is a shared feed. You find useful things, add your own, and ask when you need help. That's the whole idea.

⌕

Find

Search the feed or filter by category to see what people are sharing.

✦

Share

Post an opportunity or a useful resource in under a minute.

↗

Ask

Make a request and let the community point you in the right direction.

✎

Stay in control

Edit or delete your own posts any time. Nobody else can.

Safe by design

Your posts, your control.

🔒

Only you can change your posts

Every post is linked to its author, and the server checks ownership before any edit or delete.

⚠

Check before you trust

Be cautious with requests for money or personal information, and confirm details with the original source.

✉

Log in your way

Use your username or your email. Passwords are stored securely, never in plain text.
